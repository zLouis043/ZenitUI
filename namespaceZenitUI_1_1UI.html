◆ ContextMenu()

std::shared_ptr< Popup > ZenitUI::UI::ContextMenu ( std::vector< std::pair< std::string, std::function< void()> > > items)
inline

Convenience factory: a vertical context menu popup.

Each item is a full-width, left-aligned button. Clicking an item closes the popup and fires its callback.

Parameters
itemsList of (label, callback) pairs. Empty labels produce invisible buttons; use them for separators at your own discretion (no special handling).
