◆ beginFrame()

void ZenitUI::UIContext::beginFrame ( float delta_time)
inline

Advance the frame: pull platform state and reset one-shot flags.

Called once at the top of Layout::runFrame. On viewport or safe-area change, viewportGeneration is bumped so every cached style key changes and everything re-resolves.

Parameters
delta_timeSeconds since the previous frame.
