Detailed Description

Reference IRenderer backed by Raylib / rlgl.

Owns the mapping from opaque ZenitUI handles (TextureHandle, FontHandle, EffectHandle, TargetHandle) to native Raylib GPU objects, and maintains software stacks for transforms, scissor clips, active shaders, and nested off-screen render targets.

Note
Must be constructed after Raylib's InitWindow() and destroyed (or have its resources unloaded) before CloseWindow().
Examples
01_hello_world/main.cpp, 02_layouts/main.cpp, 03_widgets/main.cpp, 04_animations/main.cpp, 04b_imperative_animations/main.cpp, 05_zstyle/main.cpp, 06_zmarkup/main.cpp, 07_effects/main.cpp, 07b_effects_stress/main.cpp, 08_portals/main.cpp, 09_custom_widget/main.cpp, 10_canvas/main.cpp, 11_ref_list/main.cpp, 12_bindable/main.cpp, and 13_prefabs/main.cpp.
