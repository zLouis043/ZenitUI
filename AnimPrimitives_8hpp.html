Detailed Description

Low-level animation types: imperative tracks, CSS keyframes, and the per-instance active animation state.

Two animation systems coexist:

  • Imperative (UIAnimation, AnimState): driven by code, one track per property, applied per-frame by AnimationPlayer::tickImperative. Used by Modal::show, Popup::openInternal, and any component that needs runtime-controlled tweens.
  • Declarative CSS (KeyframeAnimation, ActiveCssAnimation): driven by the animations field of a Style, sampled from the theme's @keyframes registry, and overlaid onto the resolved style at draw time by Anim::overlayCssStyle / Anim::overlayCssComputed.

The two systems share easing (Easing.hpp) and style interpolation (Style.hpp::lerpProp and friends) but are otherwise independent.
