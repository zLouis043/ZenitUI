Detailed Description

template<size_t MaxVertices, size_t MaxIndices>
struct ZenitUI::Geometry2D::StaticMesh< MaxVertices, MaxIndices >

Fixed-capacity stack mesh returned by Geometry2D builders.

Avoids heap allocations during per-frame tessellation while exposing std::span views directly consumable by IRenderer::drawGeometry.
