Detailed Description

Backend-independent text shaping (HarfBuzz), glyph rasterization (FreeType), dynamic atlas packing, layout, hit-testing, and batched rendering.

Designed to live as a value member inside UIContext (zero global state). FreeType and HarfBuzz headers are strictly private to src/TextEngine.cpp via PIMPL.
