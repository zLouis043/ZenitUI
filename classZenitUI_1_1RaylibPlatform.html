Detailed Description

Reference IPlatform backed by Raylib window, input, and clipboard APIs.

Translates Raylib's physical screen coordinates into ZenitUI logical coordinates by dividing by dpiScale(), and maps Raylib key/mouse states into ZenitUI's per-frame PointerState and InputEvents.
